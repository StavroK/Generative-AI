# Scrum Operating Cadence for AI Delivery

## Sprint Planning
Include product backlog plus data, evaluation, security, infrastructure and operational work.

## Daily Stand-up
Focus on flow blockers, environment/data dependencies and decisions—not status theater.

## Backlog Refinement
Ensure AI stories have measurable acceptance criteria. Avoid stories such as "improve the model" without an evaluation target.

## Sprint Review / Demo
Demo both user behavior and evidence: quality metrics, failure examples, latency, cost or controls when relevant.

## Retrospective
Examine delivery process plus AI-specific sources of rework: data quality, unclear evaluation criteria, model variability and environment constraints.
