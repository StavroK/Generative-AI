# Delivery Roadmap

| Phase | Weeks | Outcome | Exit Criteria |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Discovery | 1-2 | Scope, users, data, KPIs | Sponsor + architecture alignment |
| Foundation | 3-4 | Secure AWS baseline | IAM/KMS/logging reviewed |
| RAG MVP | 5-7 | End-to-end Q&A | Retrieval + citations operational |
| Evaluation | 8-9 | Quality benchmark | KPI thresholds met or remediation agreed |
| UAT | 10 | Business validation | UAT sign-off |
| Production Readiness | 11 | Operational controls | Release gate approved |
| Pilot Launch | 12 | Limited production | Monitoring + support active |
