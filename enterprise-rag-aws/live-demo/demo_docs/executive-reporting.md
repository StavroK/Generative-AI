# Executive AI Portfolio Reporting

Executive reporting is designed around decisions, not activity lists.

The Northstar steering report summarizes:

- portfolio health;
- milestone confidence;
- approved versus forecast budget;
- executive decisions required;
- cross-project dependencies;
- top risks;
- AI quality or operational measures for projects approaching production.

A Yellow project requires management attention but is still recoverable within the planning horizon.

A Red project means a major commitment is unlikely to be met without executive intervention, re-baselining, or scope change.

Every executive escalation should include the decision required, recommended options, consequence of delay, and accountable owner.
