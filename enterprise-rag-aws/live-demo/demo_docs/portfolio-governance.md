# AI Portfolio Governance Standard

The Northstar AI portfolio is reviewed at three levels:

- weekly workstream delivery review;
- biweekly cross-project dependency review;
- monthly executive steering committee.

Projects report scope, milestone confidence, budget forecast, dependencies, RAID items, and AI-specific quality or operational measures.

Yellow status indicates management attention is required but recovery remains feasible within the planning horizon.

Red status indicates a major commitment is unlikely to be met without executive intervention, re-baselining, or scope change.
